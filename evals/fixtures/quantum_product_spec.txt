Quantum AI Intelligence Suite — Internal Product Specification (v1.4)

Overview
Quantum is a unified AI workspace. Its purpose is to let a user ingest
documents, ask questions grounded in those documents, and convert the
answers into artifacts (summaries, flashcards, action plans, charts).

Core modules
1. Chat — streaming conversational interface with persistent history.
2. Knowledge Base — a per-user library of uploaded documents. Supported
   upload types: PDF, DOCX, TXT, Markdown, CSV, and JSON. Maximum upload
   size is 10 MB. Files flagged "temporary" are deleted after 24 hours.
3. RAG pipeline — documents are split into overlapping chunks of 1000
   characters with 200 characters of overlap, embedded, and retrieved by
   cosine similarity over a vector index. Retrieval returns the top 3
   chunks by default (RAG_TOP_K) and drops matches scoring below 0.5.
4. Studio — compares two or three documents side by side and generates
   flashcards from a single document.
5. Executive Assistant — produces an executive summary, action items,
   strategic gaps, and key risks as structured output.
6. Insight Engine — extracts entities, relationships, topics, a sentiment
   timeline, and SEO metrics from arbitrary text.

Security model
- All API routes except register, login, and OAuth callbacks require a
  valid JWT access token. Access tokens expire after 24 hours; refresh
  tokens after 15 days.
- Every database query that touches user data is scoped by the
  authenticated user's id. Cross-user access returns 404, not 403.
- Rate limits: 50 requests per 15 minutes on authentication routes and
  100 requests per 15 minutes on AI routes.
- Uploaded files are never served over a public path; they are streamed
  through an authenticated, ownership-checked endpoint.

Operational notes
- The embedding model is gemini-embedding-2 configured at 768 dimensions.
  Chunks store the model name and dimension count so stale embeddings can
  be detected and re-embedded.
- The chat model defaults to gemini-2.5-flash with ordered fallbacks.
- Ingestion currently embeds chunks in batches of 5 concurrent calls.
