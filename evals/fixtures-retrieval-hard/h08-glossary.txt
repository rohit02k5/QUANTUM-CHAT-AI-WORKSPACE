Internal Glossary — Rare Terms and Names

Quenzel-Parrish estimator: an in-house statistical estimator used to detect
slow-drift in replica clocks before lease violations occur. It operates on the
inter-arrival times of heartbeat acknowledgements rather than on absolute
timestamps. See also: lease manager, incident ERR-8821-C.

Miriadne protocol: the internal name for the three-phase barrier release used by
the coherency scheduler. Phase two is where the ERR-4417-A fencepost bug lived.
The name predates the current team and appears only in this document and in
scheduler source comments.

Tolliver's bound: a rule of thumb (named after the engineer who proposed it)
that read skew above 0.5% under nominal load always indicates a barrier or lease
fault, never a network partition.

Fenwick-Gray checksum trailer: the 64-byte integrity trailer appended to each
assembled chunk by the ingestion pipeline; it is the structure corrupted in
incident ERR-1093-F.
