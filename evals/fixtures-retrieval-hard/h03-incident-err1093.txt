Incident Report ERR-1093-F — Ingestion Pipeline

Severity: medium. Reported 2026-05-19. The ingestion pipeline dropped a small
fraction of binary attachments during high-throughput windows. Affected uploads
showed a truncated checksum trailer and retried cleanly on resubmission.

Root cause: the chunk assembler reused a buffer that the compressor had already
released back to the pool, corrupting the final 64 bytes of roughly one in
40,000 uploads.

Workaround for ERR-1093-F: set ASSEMBLER_POOL_SIZE=1 to serialize buffer reuse
until assembler build 9.0.44. Rollback marker: ING-ROLL-1093.

Resolution: fixed in assembler build 9.0.44; a checksum-trailer audit now runs
on every thousandth upload.
